Where clinic rollups actually happen.

Last checked: November 2, 2026

A rollup is three stories at once: physicians leaving independent practice, sponsors paying for the practices they leave, and regulators deciding where consolidation becomes dominance. The links below cover all three — the ownership surveys that count the sellers, the deal reporting that prices the transactions, the antitrust coverage that marks the boundary, and the specialty evidence from radiology and dentistry, the two fields where the rollup has run longest.

Ownership and consolidation data

Berkowitz Law Firm — Trends from the 2024 Physician Practice Benchmark Survey

Summary of the AMA survey: 42.2% of physicians in private practice in 2024, down from 60.1% in 2012; ownership stakes down to 35.4%; hospital-owned practices up to 34.5%; private-equity-owned practices up to 6.5%.

Physicians Advocacy Institute and Avalere Health — Physician Employment Trends and Practice Acquisitions, 2018–2026

Long-running study of practice acquisitions: 82.0% of physicians employed by hospitals or corporate entities as of January 2026; 63.9% of practices owned; 85,000 additional practices acquired over eight years.

Advisory Board — Physician Acquisitions Are Up, Raising Questions About Costs and Care

Coverage of the Government Accountability Office review of physician consolidation: at least 47% of physicians employed by or affiliated with hospital systems in 2024, up from under 30% in 2012.

Becker's ASC — 50 Stats Behind the Physician Consolidation Wave

Fifty statistics from 2025 studies, including the Health Affairs estimate that private-equity acquisitions of physician groups rose more than 600% from 2012 to 2021.

Deal economics and private equity

Healthcare Dive — Healthcare Private Equity Dealmaking Boosted by IT in 2025

Bain & Company's Global Healthcare Private Equity Report 2026: record $191 billion in 2025 deal value, 445 buyouts, provider-services value up 57% to $62 billion, exit value $156 billion.

Health Value Group — Trends in Physician Practice Acquisitions & Valuation Multiples

Physician-practice transaction counts (245 in 2019 to 535 in 2023, Irvin Levin data) and the Scope Research multiple sample (median 13.0x across 33 deals, May 2022–May 2024), with earnout and rollover structuring notes.

Dental Practice Insider — DSO vs Private Practice: The 2026 Industry Landscape

Dental rollup economics: DSO affiliation 16.1% in 2024, up from 7.2% in 2015; reported multiples 5–8x EBITDA for add-ons and 9–11x at platform level (FOCUS Investment Banking).

Axia Growth — Dental Practice M&A

Dentistry as the most mature practice rollup: DSO affiliation, large-platform scale (Heartland Dental, over 1,960 locations), and 2026 acquisition expectations from DSO surveys.

Regulation and antitrust

Becker's ASC — What the USAP-FTC Settlement Means for ASC Anesthesia Contracting

The FTC's case against U.S. Anesthesia Partners: the 2023 rollup allegations, the separate Welsh Carson settlement, the April 2026 agreement in principle, and the 180-day implementation stay.

Becker's Physician Leadership — The Physician Acquisition Controversies Shaping 2026

Five controversies in physician deals, led by the USAP settlement and its alleged 44%–70% Texas market shares, with the stealth-consolidation template regulators now apply.

Becker's ASC — Physician Trainees Growing Wary of PE

The other side of the seller pipeline: younger surgeons growing skeptical of giving up practice control after a decade of private equity deals and consolidation.

Specialty evidence

Radiology Business — Thousands of Radiologists Now Work for Private Equity

Radiology rollup counts: 151 private-equity acquisitions from 2013 to 2023, and about 12% of radiologists in PE-backed entities by December 2023, up from 1% in 2013.

Becker's Dental Review — DSO Affiliation Doubles as Practice Ownership Hits a 20-Year Low

TUSK Practice Sales Q4 2026 Dental Market Report notes: DSO affiliation 7.2% to 16.1% since 2015, ownership down to 73%, and 26.5% affiliation among dentists up to 10 years out of school.
